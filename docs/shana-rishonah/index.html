How every package works

Delivery and receiving
We order from Israeli stores and have everything delivered to your apartment. If you give us access (a key, a code, or someone to let us in), we can have the deliveries placed inside; on The Full Nest we receive and unpack everything ourselves. Delivery timing is agreed with you in the quote.
Installation and assembly
Installation or assembly of specific items (appliances, furniture) is priced per item and confirmed in your quote — never assumed.
Optional extras
Anything not in your package — extra furniture, a different appliance, more linen — can be added; each extra is quoted separately.
Not included
Flights, your apartment rental or lease, and anything not listed in your confirmed quote.
Timing
Planning guidance: send your request at least 3 weeks before arrival (6 weeks for The Full Nest) so orders and deliveries can be arranged. Exact timing is confirmed with your quote and depends on store lead times.
Quotes and payment
Tell us what you need. We discuss the details and confirm your package and price in writing before any payment. You pay by PayPal or Israeli bank transfer only after accepting the quote. Sending the form is an inquiry, not a booking.
