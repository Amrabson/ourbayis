Questions

  1. 01Is this what everyone spends?

    No. It is a list of basics at mid-range store prices. People spend less when they receive gifts or hand-me-downs, and more when they choose premium brands.

  2. 02Does it include furniture?

    No. Furniture and mattresses are not on this list. It covers the first week, kitchen basics, Shabbos hosting, bedding and bath, and, if you pick that starting point, appliances.

  3. 03Are appliances worth bringing from abroad?

    It depends on voltage, plugs, warranty and the brand. The guide goes through it item by item.

    Appliances: bring them or buy in Israel →

  4. 04How do guests help with this?

    Put these items on a registry. Each one can be gifted by a guest, and the bigger ones can be chipped in toward by several people.
