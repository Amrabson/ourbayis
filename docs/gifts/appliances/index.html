Good to know

  • Israeli sockets and plugs are built for 230 V, so check that an appliance suits them before you bring one from abroad.
  • For large appliances, delivery and installation are usually arranged with the store: ask what is included and book a date that fits your move.
  • Check that the warranty comes from the Israeli importer, since that is who handles repairs here, and keep the receipt.
