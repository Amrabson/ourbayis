Good to know

  • Israeli bed sizes are quoted in centimetres, and 160×200 is the common double; single beds are narrower, often around 90 cm wide.
  • Measure your mattress before buying sheets and covers, because the same named size can differ a little between brands.
  • Fitted sheets list the mattress depth they fit, so check it if your mattress is thick.
