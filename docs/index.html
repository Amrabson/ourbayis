How it works

  1. 01

    Build your list

    Pick from a catalog of things a home in Israel actually uses — from a platta to a full dinnerware set — or add anything from any store.

    For guests No account needed. Open the couple's link in English or Hebrew.

  2. 02

    Share one link

    Send it by WhatsApp, email, or on your invitation. It works the same for guests in Israel and abroad.

    For guests Pick a gift and reserve it so nobody doubles up.

  3. 03

    Gifts reach you in Israel

    Guests order from stores that deliver in Israel, or send money straight to your own payment link. Nothing passes through OurBayis.

    For guests Complete your purchase or transfer, then let the couple know it's on its way.

  4. Shabbos in your own home

    The kettle is on the counter, the platta goes on Friday afternoon, and everyone who helped knows their gift arrived.

Two ways to give

A gift from an Israeli store

  • The guest orders from the store the couple linked, in shekels.
  • It's delivered inside Israel, so nothing is shipped from abroad.
  • Everyone sees what's already reserved, so nothing is bought twice.

Money straight to the couple

  • Through the couple's own PayPal, Stripe, Bit or PayBox link.
  • For a specific gift, or simply towards the new home.
  • Guests abroad never have to face an Israeli checkout.

OurBayis never touches, holds or processes the money.

Questions

  1. 01Do you handle the money?

    No. Store gifts go through the retailer's own checkout; cash gifts go through the couple's own PayPal, Stripe, Bit or PayBox link. OurBayis never touches or holds guest money.

  2. 02Can guests abroad use it without Hebrew — and can Israeli guests use it in Hebrew?

    Yes to both. The whole site works in English and Hebrew, and every registry can be viewed in either language regardless of who created it.

  3. 03Can guests pay in dollars?

    It depends on the couple's payment link provider. Amounts shown on the site are estimates based on a published exchange rate, not a live quote.

  4. 04Is my registry public?

    Unlisted by default — anyone with the link can open it, but it isn't searchable. You can make it discoverable any time in your registry settings.

  5. 05What does "Reserved" mean?

    A guest has said they intend to buy or send this gift. It isn't a purchase confirmation — that comes when the guest reports it, and the couple confirms receipt.

  6. 06Is Shana Rishonah part of the registry?

    No — it's a separate setup service, quoted individually per couple, for anyone arriving in Israel for a year or for good.
