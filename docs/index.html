How it works

For couples

Build your list → Share one link → Gifts arrive, or money reaches you directly.

1

Build your list

Pick from our curated catalog of Israeli-apartment essentials — from a platta to a full dinnerware set — or add anything from any store.

2

Share one link

Send your registry by WhatsApp, email, or on your wedding invitation. Guests anywhere in the world can open it — in English or Hebrew.

3

Gifts arrive in Israel

Guests buy from stores that deliver in Israel, or send a cash gift straight to you. No customs, no shlepping duplicates from America.

For guests

Open the link → Pick a gift → Buy from the store or send money, then tell the couple you did.

1

No account needed. Switch to English or Hebrew at the top of the page.

2

3

Buy or send, then tell them

Buy it at the linked store, or send money to the couple directly. Either way, a tap lets them know it's on its way.

Questions

Do you handle the money?

No. Store gifts go through the retailer's own checkout; cash gifts go through the couple's own PayPal, Stripe, Bit or PayBox link. OurBayis never touches or holds guest money.

Can guests abroad use it without Hebrew?

Yes — the whole site works in English, and every registry can be viewed in English or Hebrew regardless of who created it.

Can guests pay in dollars?

It depends on the couple's payment link provider. Amounts shown on the site are estimates based on a published exchange rate, not a live quote.

Is my registry public?

Unlisted by default — anyone with the link can open it, but it isn't searchable. You can make it discoverable any time in your registry settings.

What does "Reserved" mean?

A guest has said they intend to buy or send this gift. It isn't a purchase confirmation — that comes when the guest reports it, and the couple confirms receipt.

Is Shana Rishonah part of the registry?

No — it's a separate concierge service, quoted individually per couple.
