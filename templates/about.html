OurBayis was born from a simple problem: couples building a home in Israel — especially Americans and American-Israelis — had no gift registry that actually works here. The American registries ship to America; the Israeli stores are a headache for guests abroad.

So we built a registry that speaks both languages: the couple picks the things an Israeli apartment really needs, and the aunt in New Jersey opens one link, clicks a gift, and she's done — all in English, with dollar estimates.

Gifts are bought directly from the stores, and cash gifts go directly to the couple's own PayPal. We never touch your money. The site is supported by affiliate links, advertising, and our Shana Rishonah setup service.

Hatzlacha building your bayis — mazel tov! 🏠
