Most gift registries were built for one country. A couple setting up in Yerushalayim ends up with a list that ships a 110-volt kettle from New Jersey, sheets cut for an American queen bed instead of an Israeli 160×200, and a checkout that a grandmother in Johannesburg can't follow in Hebrew and a cousin in Bnei Brak can't follow in English.

OurBayis is a registry for that apartment. The catalog is Israeli: a meicham, a platta, milchig and fleishig sets, 160×200 linen, appliances with Israeli plugs. Each item has a shekel price and, wherever we've found one, a store that delivers here; a price we've checked at the store shows the date. The couple shares one link; family and friends open it in English or Hebrew, reserve a gift so it isn't bought twice, and buy it from the store or send money to the couple's own PayPal, Bit or PayBox.

We never see the money. Store gifts are paid to the store, and cash gifts go straight to the couple's own payment link. The site pays for itself with affiliate links, a few ads, and a separate setup service for couples arriving from abroad. Questions, or a store we should add? {{ t('nav_contact') }}.

Hatzlacha building your bayis, and mazel tov.
