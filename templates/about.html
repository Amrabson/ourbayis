OurBayis was born from a simple problem: couples building a home in Israel — chassanim and kallahs, new olim, and couples coming for shana rishonah from the US, UK, South Africa, Australia, Canada and beyond — had no gift registry that actually works here. Registries built for another country ship there; Israeli stores aren't always easy for guests giving from far away.

So we built a registry that speaks both languages. The couple picks the things a home in Israel actually uses — from a platta to Israeli-size linen — and shares one link. Family and friends anywhere, in English or Hebrew, reserve a gift, buy it from the linked store or send the couple the money, then let the couple know it's on its way.

Store gifts are bought directly from the store, and cash gifts go directly to the couple's own payment link. We never touch your money. The site is supported by affiliate links, advertising, and our separate Shana Rishonah setup service. Questions? {{ t('nav_contact') }}.

Hatzlacha building your bayis — mazel tov! 🏠
