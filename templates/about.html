OurBayis was born from a simple problem: couples building a home in Israel — olim from the US, UK, South Africa, Australia, Canada and beyond — had no gift registry that actually works here. Registries built for one country ship there; Israeli stores aren't always easy for guests calling in from far away.

So we built a registry that speaks both languages: the couple picks the things an Israeli apartment really needs, and family or friends anywhere in the world — in English or Hebrew — open one link, click a gift, and they're done, with prices in shekels and an approximate conversion.

Gifts are bought directly from the stores, and cash gifts go directly to the couple's own payment link. We never touch your money. The site is supported by affiliate links, advertising, and our Shana Rishonah setup service.

Hatzlacha building your bayis — mazel tov! 🏠
